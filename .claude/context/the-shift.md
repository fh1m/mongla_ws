# The Shift — Pixhawk + Jetson → SROT + AI HAT

**The document lives at [`docs/the-shift.md`](../../docs/the-shift.md)**, published at
<https://fh1m.github.io/mongla_ws/the-shift.html>.

It is written for a reader with no robotics background: what a flight controller is, why an
AUV needs both a real-time controller and a companion computer, what the old four-computer
stack could not do, and what the SROT board (firmware **Hengla**) plus a Raspberry Pi 5 with
a Hailo-8 AI HAT replaced it with. It also names the four repositories, the firmware
development team lead (**Rakibul Islam**), and the pull-request culture between hardware,
firmware and this codebase.

One copy on purpose: the public page *is* the document, so the community version and the
agent-facing version cannot drift apart.
