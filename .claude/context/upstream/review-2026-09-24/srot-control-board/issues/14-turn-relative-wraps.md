# TURN: a relative turn wraps to the shortest path (+270° turns -90°, +360° completes instantly), and the constant-rate turn has no deceleration

Part of the 2026-09-24 ecosystem review (tracking issue in fh1m/mongla_ws, link to follow).

## Summary
In relative mode (`p4=0`), `SROT_MOVE TURN` computes `target = wrapPi(cur + primary)`, and the turn phase then drives `wrapPi(target − yaw)` to zero. So a relative turn of more than 180° goes the **other way**, a turn of exactly 360° (or any multiple) completes at once without moving, and progress is computed against the wrapped span. `JETSON_COMMS.md` says relative mode "turns ±p2° from the current heading". The turn is also bang-bang: full commanded rate until the error is under 0.03 rad, then an instant handover to heading hold, so the vehicle overshoots in proportion to its yaw momentum.

## Failure scenario
1. A mission does a "style" 360° spin, or a search pattern commands `turn +270` to face a side wall by turning right.
2. `+360`: the target equals the current heading, the error is 0 on the first tick, and the move reports ACCEPTED at 100 % after a 0° rotation.
3. `+270`: the target is 90° to the left, so the vehicle turns left 90°. Any tether, camera sweep or scoring rule that depends on the direction of rotation is wrong, and so is the host's dead-reckoned yaw if it integrated the commanded sign.
4. On any turn at 45°/s, the rate demand stays at full until 1.7° from the target and then drops to zero. With the yaw inertia of a T200 hull, it typically overshoots by several degrees, and heading hold has to pull it back. The completion ACK arrives before the vehicle has settled.

## Suggested fix
- **Relative mode:** track the accumulated rotation, not a wrapped target:
```cpp
s_turn_goal = primary_rad;  s_turn_done = 0;  s_prev_yaw = cur_yaw;   // at start
// each tick:
s_turn_done += wrapPi(yaw - s_prev_yaw); s_prev_yaw = yaw;
float err = s_turn_goal - s_turn_done;                                  // unwrapped
```
  Complete when `|err| < tol`, and lock `d.yaw_lock = wrapPi(start + goal)`. Absolute mode keeps shortest-path behaviour, as documented.
- **Deceleration:** limit the rate by the remaining angle, `rate = min(s_yaw_rate, sqrt(2*a_max*|err|))`, with `a_max` from a param such as `MOVE_YAW_ACCEL`. Complete only when `|err| < tol && |gz| < rate_tol`, so the ACK means the vehicle has settled.
- Update `JETSON_COMMS.md` in either case.

## How to verify
- **Unit test of `movement` with a simulated yaw integrator:** `+360` must rotate a full 360° with progress passing through 0.5 at 180°. `+270` must rotate positive. `-450` must rotate −450°.
- **Bench, gimbal or turntable:** log `ATTITUDE.yaw` and `yawspeed` through a 90° turn. The overshoot must be under 2°, and the ACK must follow the vehicle settling.
- **Injection test:** restore `wrapPi` on the relative target and confirm the +360 test fails.

## Severity: Medium
